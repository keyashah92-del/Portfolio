Hi, I'm Keya!

I find the why behind the buy.

Consumer insights · Marketing analytics · Brand storytelling

I'm a perfectionist and a hustler. I set tough goals, I hate under-delivering, and I do my best work with a full plate. While I'm on one task I'm already planning the next three, which is how I find my real capacity.

I overanalyze outcomes because I want my work to hold up. Not knowing something while I'm in the room is what I like least, and it keeps me curious and always asking why.

Reading consumers and predicting what they'll do next is my thing. It's what drew me to psychology, and it's what I'm building a marketing career around.

Outside of work, you'll usually find me exploring what's new in the city, hunting for low-key sunset spots to share, and chasing down food I love.

MS Marketing Analytics student at Baruch College. Psychology is my soul, AI is my edge, advertising is my stage.

  4. Bloc AI Engine

    An answer-engine optimization tool for a pharma advertising agency

    Brief
    Search is shifting to AI answer engines. Pharma brands need to know how they show up there and whether what is said about them is compliant.
    Insight
    The large agency groups are building their own AI platforms for this. I researched what Havas, Publicis and WPP have built to see where a smaller agency could stand out. Pharma label compliance emerged as the clearest opening, with a phased view of what is buildable and human review kept as a hard limit.
    Output
    A competitive landscape document, a pitch deck, an elevator brief and an interactive prototype that walks through a Scan, Trace and Flag flow.
    Proof
    Shared with agency leadership and received well. It connected to the agency's own agentic AI and medical-legal-review work.

    The Agency of the Future

    Apprenticeship capstone: an AI hub concept called The Orange Line

    Brief
    Imagine how a pharma market access agency should work as AI changes the business, and what it would take to get there.
    Concept
    The Orange Line, a shared AI hub that serves every department, with outputs such as a same-day Smart Brief. The case was built around the same team handling more accounts, an estimated 20 to 30 percent more.
    Approach
    Mapped the benefit to each department, tied it to the agency's strategic priorities and laid out a phased rollout. Use cases were tied to what colleagues said in interviews, and anything not yet confirmed was marked as proposed. Human medical-legal-regulatory review stays a fixed limit.
    My role
    Development strategy, on a team of five. I wrote the project management use cases: auto-generated status updates and meeting summaries, instant visibility into timelines and risks, first-pass project plans, and one view that pulls updates from every stakeholder.
